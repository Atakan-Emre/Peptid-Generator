generate_peptides_with_best_model(...) içinde SA yerine MOCO’yu bağlama

Şu bloğu:

optimizer = PeptideOptimizer(
    model=model,
    model_type=model_type,
    score_mean=score_mean,
    score_std=score_std,
    initial_temp=initial_temp,
    cooling_rate=cooling_rate,
    max_iterations=max_iterations
)


şununla değiştir:

optimizer = MocoCEMPeptideOptimizer(
    model=model,
    model_type=model_type,
    score_mean=score_mean,
    score_std=score_std,

    # MOCO budget
    max_iterations=350,       # SA 3000 yerine genelde 200-600 arası yeterli
    batch_size=128,
    elite_frac=0.12,
    alpha=0.18,

    # exploration -> exploitation
    tau_start=1.8,
    tau_end=0.65,
    tau_schedule="linear",

    # stability
    base_mix=0.05,
    min_prob=1e-6,

    # elite local improvement
    use_local_search=True,
    local_search_elite_frac=0.25,
    local_steps=25,

    seed=1
)
